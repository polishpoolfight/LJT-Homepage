## About Me

I am a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present)
  - HKUST NLP Group; Supervisor: Prof. Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020–2024; graduated June 2024)
  - Previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU.
- **Honors:** Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

## Research Experience

- **Research Intern**, MINIMAX (February 2025–Present)
- **Research Intern**, Tencent WXG (June 2024–September 2024); Advisor: Zifei Shan
- **Research Intern**, Shanghai AI Lab (June 2023–December 2023); Advisor: Prof. Yu Cheng

## Skills and Research Interests

- Natural language processing and machine learning
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
